05 / GOOD TO KNOW

A FEW
FIRST QUESTIONS.

A starting point for your conversation.
Details are agreed with your coach.

Do I need experience to get started?+

Your starting point is part of the conversation. Share your experience and any concerns so the first session can be planned around you.

Which training format should I choose?+

Choose the option that interests you most, or select “Help me choose” in the form below. Your coach can help you explore the options.

What should we discuss before a session?+

Your goals, current activity, availability and any relevant limitations. Confirm the location, equipment and session details before attending.

Can I book through this website?+

This is a portfolio design concept. You can try the form, but it does not make a booking or send your information.
